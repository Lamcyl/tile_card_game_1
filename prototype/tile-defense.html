How to play
  1. Enemies sit at the top. Each shows ♥ HP and ⚔ ATK (the damage it deals you).
  2. Place your whole army on the bottom 3 rows, then press Fight.
  3. Attackers fire upward, front row first, left to right. R is how many tiles up they reach.
  4. Supporters boost the attacker their arrow points at. All + bonuses apply first, then ×.
  5. Survivors hit your hero for their ATK. A Shield absorbs damage in its column.
  6. Survivors enrage (+1 ⚔) at the end of each turn. Rearrange and fight again.
↑ Straight: first enemy in its column
⇈ Pierce: every enemy in its column
↖↑↗ Spread: first enemy in 3 columns
Overkill: extra damage from a straight or spread hit carries to the enemy behind
🛢️ Barrel: explodes for 2 damage to neighbours. Doesn't need to be destroyed.
🛡 Armor: every hit deals that much less
