Greenvale: the whole region

All eight sub-areas stitched onto one shared 36 x 58 tile grid (about 56 px tiles, no grid lines). Drag to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom, double-tap to zoom in. The jump buttons frame a 390 px-wide phone screen (7 tiles across) so you can judge how it feels while playing. Mockup only, nothing here is wired into the game.

overview

Overview shows the dashed sub-area outlines and name labels; play zoom is the same drawing at 1:1 (390 px = 7 tiles).

Route: Camp (south edge) → 1 Sunny Meadows → west gate into 2 Old Oak Wood → north gate into 3 Bamboo Valley → east gate (stream mouth) into 4 Mirror Lake → north gate up to 5 Rice Terraces → west gate into 6 Mossy Ruins → north gate into 7 Firefly Marsh → east gate into 8 Misty Highlands → stone mountain gate at the north edge, the way out to the next region.
