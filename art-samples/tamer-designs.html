Character concepts

Tamers and their monsters

Five factions, one for each region. Each faction has a tamer (the hero you pick for a run) and five monsters (the units you place on the board). Every board role from the current rules is covered. Tell me which factions, tamers and monsters you like, and what to change.

Board roles, renamed for monsters

Same rules as now; only the names change. The coloured badge stays on every monster so the puzzle is always readable.

How it reads on the board

Actual phone tile size (62 px). Wild Ember monsters on top, a mixed team below.

  • Badge (top left) shows the role. Number (bottom right) is the final attack after boosts.
  • Wild monsters show ♥ HP and ⚔ attack instead.
  • Here Emberling doubles Narwhee above it (3 × 2 = 6), and Bloomcap boosts its neighbour Podpop (2 + 1 = 3).
  • Simple, round silhouettes with big eyes stay recognisable at this size; thin details don't.

Duplicates evolve a monster

Each evolution raises its stats and changes its look: a bigger body, more thorns, a leafy crown.

What to tell me

Which factions and characters you like or dislike, whether the tamers should look older or younger, cuter or cooler, and whether the monsters should stay cute or get fiercer as they evolve. These are vector mock-ups for choosing a direction; final art would be illustrated in the chosen style.
