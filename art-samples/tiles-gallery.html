Tile map designs

Five tile-based takes on the exploration map: every tile is passable ground, an obstacle or boundary, or a tile of interest (enemy with danger skulls, chest, shrine, shop, boss gate). No paths; the hero steps to a highlighted neighbouring tile. Tell me which numbers to take further. Tell me which numbers to take further.

Watercolour squares

Soft brushed-edge square tiles on paper: trees, rock, water and wall tiles, glowing ring pads under monsters, fog as drifting cloud.

Painted hex board game

Chunky hexes on a wooden-framed game board; unexplored hexes are face-down indigo tiles with wave patterns.

3

Isometric diorama

Rounded diamond tiles with real height: cliffs, a sunken river and a raised wall gate, fading into cloud at the edges.

4

Ink grid on paper

Hairline square grid with small line-drawn icons and a single vermilion accent; fog is just stipple. Units stand out most here.

5

Chunky papercraft

Thick raised cardstock tiles with cut-out trees, round paper tokens under monsters; reachable tiles pop up in yellow, fog is crumpled grey paper.
