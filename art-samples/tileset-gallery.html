Greenvale tileset 緑の谷

2118 hand-written SVG tiles in round-3 design B (seamless watercolour terrain, crisp 3/4-view props with soft painted shading and a soft drop shadow): the common set plus Sunny Meadows, Old Oak Wood (step 2), Bamboo Valley and Mirror Lake (step 3). Base cell 64×64, tall props 64×128, large objects 128×128. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.

Tile sets

One page per set; each lists every tile by category with the autotile sets grouped by name. The pages load the SVG files by relative path, so keep the layout tileset-gallery.html, tileset/, tiles/greenvale/ together when hosting.

Common 537

Shared by the whole region: seamless ground families, the autotile sets (water shore, deep water, dirt, sand, cliff, forest), trees, rocks, fences and hedges, props, interest objects and overlays.

Sunny Meadows 318

Wildflower carpets and patch autotiles, rolling hills, farm fields with crops, farm fences and stone walls, windmill, barn, beehives, scarecrows, picnic, orchard and sunflowers, the lone blossom tree with petal drift, and a lily pond.

Old Oak Wood 360

Forest-floor variants (moss, roots, litter, ferns, glow), oaks of every age, the sacred oak and its hollow-trunk door, root arches, fungi and glowing mushrooms, fallen logs, shrine stones and a stone circle, a woodcutter camp, owls and webs, plus the meadow to forest blends.

Bamboo Valley 443

Green, golden, black and young bamboo (clumps, bushes, stands, three tree-line autotiles), a clear stream autotile with pebbly banks that flows east, stepping stones, rapids, koi, plank bridges and an arched bridge, a waterfall, thatched huts and the hut gate, a tea house, stone lanterns, shishi-odoshi and tsukubai, bamboo fences and screens, valley mist and light shafts.

Mirror Lake 460

Open water with reflections and ripples, lily pads and lotus in several states, reeds and cattails, a pebble-beach shoreline autotile, reflective deep water, islets and island autotile, lily beds, connecting jetties, rowboats and a ferry, the vermilion torii standing in the water with its reflection, boathouse gate, shrine islet, market boat, fishing nets, herons and ducks, willows, and evening sparkle.

Sample patches

Composed at random from the tiles: random grass variants side by side with no seams, ponds and lakes (shore set, deep set, lily beds), cliffs, tree lines, fenced fields, plus props and fog. Reroll for a new layout.
