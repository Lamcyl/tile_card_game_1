Firefly Marsh 蛍の沼

The marsh at dusk. Dusk-tinted grass (and half-way ramps back to day grass), peat, mud and bog ground, marsh water with duckweed, lilies, sky and firefly reflections; pool, deep-water, peat, mud, bog, reed-bed and dusk-blend autotiles (the dusk patches fade into day grass and into the Mossy Ruins moss); connecting boardwalks (plain, mossy, broken, lantern-lit) and a reed fence; willows, snags, swamp cypress, alders, reeds, cattails, irises, dusk lily pads, sunken boats, stumps in water, floating paper lanterns, lantern poles, will-o-wisps, frogs, heron silhouettes, jizo and shrine pieces; stilt huts, the stilt-hut gate (closed, open), a watchtower, pier and islet shrine; fireflies in several densities, swarms, floating lanterns, mist banks and dusk tint washes.

736 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/greenvale/firefly-marsh/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
