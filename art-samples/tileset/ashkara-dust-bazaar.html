Ashkara: Dust Bazaar 砂塵の市場

Sub-area 6 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md): a ruined caravanserai market town. Dusty brown-rose packed earth, swept and trampled lanes, road dust, straw litter, plaza slab and cobbles, glazed court tile, woven rug floors in madder red, teal and saffron, awning shade, mud-brick floor and fallen brick, ramps into the common ground, Red Wadi terracotta to the east, the Singing Erg apricot sand to the south and the Tomb Approach slab to the north; three 47-piece autotile sets (flat-roofed mud-brick blocks, plaza paving, striped awning cloth), a mud-brick market wall and a camel hitching rail (connect-4); courtyard fig and acacia shade trees, potted pomegranates and trellis vines, fallen brick, ruined wall stubs, column stumps, millstones, rubble, dry weeds, thistle and hay; awnings, hanging lamps and lamp posts, hitching posts, kneeling camels, braziers, teapots, cushions, rugs, sacks, crates, barrels, jars, banners, ovens and more; the ruined caravanserai with market court (landmark), the bazaar city gate (gate, closed and open), spice, rug, pottery, lamp and fruit stalls, rug merchants, mud-brick houses and towers, covered well courts, caravan wagons, dyer vats and chests; decals and awning shade, dust haze, cooking smoke, spice haze, lantern glow, sun shaft and dusk overlays.
