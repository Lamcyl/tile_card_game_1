Ashkara: Red Wadi 赤い涸れ谷

Sub-area 5 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md): a red sandstone canyon. Deep terracotta and ochre with blue-violet shade; terracotta shelves with strata, ochre and red sand, dry river gravel and cobbles, cracked clay, flood silt and a still green pool, ramps into the common ground, the Salt Mirror crust to the south, the Dust Bazaar earth to the west and the Sun Citadel gold to the north-east; three 47-piece autotile sets (red strata cliff, wadi bed, wadi pool), a red dry-stone wall and a rope bridge (connect-4); twisted junipers, wadi tamarisk, snags, caper, wild fig, oleander, hoodoo spires, boulders, rubble, strata slabs, balanced rock, sandstone arches, mesa buttes, scree, reeds and thornbush; rope ladders, cave mouths, flood debris, rock-cut steps, goats, jars, cairns and sconces; the rock-cut tomb facade (landmark), the rock-cut canyon door (gate, closed and open), cliff dwellings, hoodoo grove, wadi spring, flood wreck, bedouin camp, steles, rock obelisk, high-place altar and chests; decals and blue canyon shade, cliff shadow, red dust, flood mist and sun-shaft overlays.

366 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/ashkara/red-wadi/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
