Ashkara: Seven Palms 七本椰子のオアシス

Sub-area 2 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md): the oasis. Lush yellow-green grass, loam, oasis sand and a cool turquoise pool with lily pads and lotus, ramps into the common desert ground and into the Dune Shallows cream sand; four 47-piece autotile sets (lagoon pool, lush meadow, papyrus bed, irrigated field), a mud-brick irrigation ditch and an oleander hedge (connect-4); fruiting date palms, groves, tamarisk, oleander, pomegranate and fig, papyrus, cattails, shaduf, wells, fish traps, date baskets and jugs, the oasis pool ringed by seven palms (landmark), the palm-frond gate (closed and open), stalls, huts, a pavilion, fountain, dock and camp, decals and palm-shade overlays.

407 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/ashkara/seven-palms/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
