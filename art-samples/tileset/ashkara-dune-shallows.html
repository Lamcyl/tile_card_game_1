Ashkara: Dune Shallows 砂丘の浅瀬

Sub-area 1 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md): pale cream sand under a bright sky with almost no shade. Cream sand, wavelets, dry wadi puddles and clay, shell and fossil sand, scrub sprigs, ramps into the common desert ground; four 47-piece autotile sets (dune crest, dry pool, scrub patch, shell bed) and a driftwood-and-rope fence; dune scrub, saltbush, bleached brush and thorn acacia, sand-washed and half-buried rocks, wind pillars, marram and sea-oats; cairn trails, tumbleweed, whale bones, windsock poles, wagon wheels and camp debris; the half-buried caravan wagon, the waypoint cairn on the dune crest, the whale-rib arch gate (closed and open), a whale skeleton, a fossil dig, an abandoned camp and a driftwood shelter; plus lizard, shell, rut and drift decals and bright haze, spindrift and glare overlays.

360 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/ashkara/dune-shallows/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
