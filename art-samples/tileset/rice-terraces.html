Rice Terraces 棚田

Stepped paddy terraces, earth banks, channels and a waterwheel. Flooded paddies in every growth stage (bare water, young shoots, mid green, ripe gold, stubble) with reflections, azolla, tadpoles and seedling bundles; five stepped terrace autotiles whose earth banks and dry-stone retaining walls read as height steps, flat bunded paddy fields, and a lake shore that turns into an earth bank; irrigation channels, wooden flumes and drying-rack lines, a sluice gate, plank bridges, an animated-looking waterwheel (four phases), farmhouses, the white kura storehouse gate to the Mossy Ruins, scarecrows, field shrines and foxes, persimmon trees, straw stacks, ducks, egrets, straw-hat farmer decals and harvest overlays.

576 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/greenvale/rice-terraces/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
