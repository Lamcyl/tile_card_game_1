Misty Highlands 霧の高原

Ochre pasture, heather, gorse, moor, scree and rock slab ground (with ramps from the marsh and the ruins); autotiles for ochre, heather and scree patches, gorse thickets, grey ridges with height, mountain streams and a ravine; a rope bridge, prayer-flag lines, dry-stone walls and rope fences; wind-bent pines, rowan, birch, standing stones, stone circles, cairns, boulders, mountain shrines and lanterns, waterfalls, shepherd huts, goat and bird decals, and the stone mountain gate (closed, open) that leads out of Greenvale; drifting and low mist, rising ravine mist and cloud shadows.

676 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/greenvale/misty-highlands/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
