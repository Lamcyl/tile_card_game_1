Ashkara: Tomb Approach 墓への参道

Sub-area 7 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md), the one sub-area partly inside a structure: a sphinx and ram-statue avenue ending in a pylon entrance hall with an open roof. Golden sandstone with lapis and red paint and indigo shade; avenue slabs (fresh, worn, sand-filled), hall flagstones in sun, shade and black granite, painted floors (lotus, diamond, band), a gold-and-basalt inlay, drifted sand, ramps into the common ground, the Dust Bazaar slab and earth to the south and the Sun Citadel gold to the east; four 47-piece autotile sets (painted hall wall and rough tomb wall, both 3/4 view with a lit cap and a face, avenue paving, sand drift) and three connect-4 sets (painted thin wall, low sandstone wall, papyrus colonnade); date palms, tamarisk, potted papyrus, fallen blocks, column drums, toppled heads, rubble, dry scrub and sedge; braziers (lit and unlit), papyrus, lotus and palm columns, sphinx, ram and Anubis statues, obelisks, steles, sarcophagi, canopic jars, offerings, scarab seals, banners, mummy cases and more; the entrance hall (landmark, seen from the avenue and from inside), the sealed tomb door (gate, closed and open), pylon towers, the great sphinx, a seated colossus, doorways, a false door, shrine niches, sand-choked stairs, a sarcophagus chamber, altar and chests; decals with painted wall scenes, and hanging roof beam, beam shadow, sun shaft, dust mote, brazier glow and smoke overlays.
