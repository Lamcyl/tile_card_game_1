Ashkara: Singing Erg 歌う砂海

Sub-area 3 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md): a sea of rippled dunes. Warm apricot sand with violet lee shadows, wind-packed and slip-face sand, bright crest sand, gravel lag, rare dune grass, ramps into the common desert ground, the Seven Palms oasis to the south and the Salt Mirror hardpan to the east; four 47-piece autotile sets (slip-face ridge, lee hollow, dune-grass patch, ripple field), a chime-pole line and a sand-slat fence (connect-4); saxaul, ghost trees, wind acacia, desert broom, buried column tops, statue heads, crest cairns, yardangs, dune grass and singing reeds, chime poles, bone drifts, sand devils, scarab swarms, the drum and bronze bell; the Singing Dune with wind-chime poles (landmark), the twin-pillar arch gate (closed and open), a buried colonnade, a colossal statue head, a chime grove, a nomad camp, a drum platform, a beast skeleton, a crest monument and a scarab nest; plus sound-ring, track, bone and shadow decals and dune-shadow, crest-plume, shimmer and sand-veil overlays.

400 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/ashkara/singing-erg/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
