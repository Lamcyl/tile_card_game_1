Ashkara: Sun Citadel 太陽の城塞

Sub-area 8 of Ashkara (maps/DESERT.md), the last one: a golden citadel with an obelisk court rising out of a glass desert, and the region exit. White-gold light, dark ink-blue shade and obsidian; pale gold sand (the ground the Red Wadi and Tomb Approach ramps already lead to), fused glass sand and glass melt, scorched earth and cinders, black basalt, obsidian slabs, gold court flags, a polished mirror floor, gold and obsidian inlays, ink-blue shade, ramps into the common ground, Red Wadi (south) and the Tomb Approach avenue (west); four 47-piece autotile sets (gold citadel wall and basalt wall, both 3/4 view with a lit cap and a face, obsidian slab patch, fused glass pool) and two connect-4 sets (gold parapet, framed mirror screen); scorched palms, glass trees, gold thorn, basalt boulders and columns, obsidian spires and shards, glass lumps, slag, gold ore, fallen gold blocks, burnt scrub, ash tufts and glass grass; gold obelisks, solar discs, mirror stands, fire bowls, djinn statues, scorched skeletons, gold pillars, sun banners, a crystal lens stand, urns, a sundial, a gong; the golden citadel (landmark, seen from the glass desert and as the obelisk court), the golden citadel gate (region exit, closed and open), citadel towers and dome, sunfire altar, djinn colossus, mirror array, glass spires, doorways, stairs, sun chests and a burnt camp; decals (glass cracks, scorch marks, ash, embers, gold dust, cast shadows) and overlays (heat-distortion haze, sun glare, light rays, ember drift, golden haze, mirror glints, smoke, ink-blue shade).
