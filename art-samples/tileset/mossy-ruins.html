Mossy Ruins 苔むす遺跡

Moss-covered shrine ruins with flagstone, broken torii and wisps. Mossy, cracked, wet and broken flagstone ground, moss carpets, murky pools, a paving patch, moss-grown pool and terrace autotiles that continue the rice terraces uphill into a paved shrine platform, crumbled wall sets (plain, ivy, low rubble) and a flagstone path, standing and toppled pillars, stone lanterns (lit, unlit, broken, spirit-lit), jizo, komainu, gorinto and steles, broken torii, the ruined archway gate to the Firefly Marsh, a collapsed shrine, overgrown stairs, ivy, vines, puddles, will-o-the-wisp glows, mist and light shafts.

435 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/greenvale/mossy-ruins/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
