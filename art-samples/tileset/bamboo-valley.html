Bamboo Valley 竹の谷

Green, golden, black and young bamboo (clumps, bushes, stands, three tree-line autotiles), a clear stream autotile with pebbly banks that flows east, stepping stones, rapids, koi, plank bridges and an arched bridge, a waterfall, thatched huts and the hut gate, a tea house, stone lanterns, shishi-odoshi and tsukubai, bamboo fences and screens, valley mist and light shafts. The stream-mouth set blends the stream into Mirror Lake.

443 tiles. Shown at 2× on paper cards; tiles are loaded from ../tiles/greenvale/bamboo-valley/. Spec in tiles/TILESET.md.
