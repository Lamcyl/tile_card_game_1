# Slavic set — brief


**Fixed (the only hard constraints):**
- Black-and-white ink illustration, chibi proportions (big head, small body).
- Palette: ink `#1b1a1a`, paper `#f6f2e9`, greys `#d4cec3` `#9a948b` `#55514c`, plus ONE accent: vermilion `#c8352b` or gold `#c9a23a`.
- `viewBox="0 0 256 256"`, transparent background, hand-written SVG, no `<text>`, no raster images.
- Two versions per creature: **cute** and **fierce**. They should feel like genuinely different takes, not the same drawing with a new face.
- Must still read as a silhouette when shrunk to 64 px (it becomes a game tile).

**Everything else is your call:** pose, angle (3/4, side, dynamic, curled up, mid-leap), body shape, proportions, composition, line weight, how much detail, how you show "fierce". Be bold and give each creature its own personality and shape language. Don't make them all the same squat standing body.

The bar is "would someone want to collect this?" Draw like an illustrator designing a character for a gacha or monster-collecting game, not like someone filling a template.

**Slavic flavour (optional inspiration, not rules):** embroidered folk patterns (rushnyk/vyshyvanka motifs), birch forests, onion domes, carved wood, wheat sheaves, swamp reeds, winter frost, red-on-white folk-art accents. Gods treated respectfully.
