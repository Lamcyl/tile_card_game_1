# Bestiary

Master list of mythical beasts, monsters, spirits and animals from many cultures, for the game's units and enemies. Art status comes from `art-samples/SUMMARY.md`, `art-samples/picks-data.mjs` and each set's README; game use from `prototype/js/world-data.js`, `docs/ENEMY_REVAMP.md` and `prototype/assets/units/`.

**Inclusion rule.** This list favours folkloric monsters, beasts, spirits, undead, constructs and real animals that are commonly depicted in games, books and media and are not objects of worship. Deities and sacred figures of living religions or living traditions are not added (no Hindu gods, mounts or avatars, no Orisha or Vodun, no Islamic figures, saints or angels, no kami, no Native American or Aboriginal sacred beings). The Native North American and Australian Aboriginal cultures are omitted entirely for that reason. The only deity entries are ones already drawn in the existing art sets; they stay in the tables, marked "⚠ deity – review", and are collected in one section near the end so they can be decided on. Descriptions follow the traditional sources; where the origin is uncertain the entry is left out.

## Legend

- Art status: ★ chosen pick, ○ drawn but not picked, ✕ rejected or dropped, blank = not drawn. Fenrir, Kraken, Vodyanoy and Bastet are ✕ dropped and must not be redrawn; the Chinese guardians Long, Fenghuang, Baihu and Xuanwu are also ✕.
- ◆ = already in the prototype (used in a current enemy list or has a file in `prototype/assets/units/`).
- Kind: beast, monster, spirit, undead, deity-beast, animal, construct. Habitat terms: meadow, forest, bamboo, lake, marsh, highland, desert, oasis, saltflat, canyon, tomb, ice, volcano, coast, sea, sky, underworld, city, river, mountain, cave, jungle, steppe, ruins, village, island.
- Note column: "⚠ deity – review" marks a deity shown in an animal or composite form.
