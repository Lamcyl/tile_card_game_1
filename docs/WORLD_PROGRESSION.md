# World Progression: Regions 1 to 10

Status: proposal. Design data only; no game code changed. Data: `docs/data/regions.json` (every region and sub-area has a stable `id` and `"status": "proposed"`). Check: `node docs/data/check-regions.mjs` (every roster and boss base name exists in `BESTIARY.md`, is Malevolent or Ambivalent, and is not in "Removed for sensitivity").

Inputs: DESIGN_V2.md (units, 9 elements, 17 reactions, families, Rend), BESTIARY.md (variants, families, terrain index), ENEMY_REVAMP.md Part B (Greenvale and Ashkara battle scripts), GAME_DESIGN.md 24.8 (level rule), IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md (8 compass directions, structure regions). Counts: **10 regions, 100 sub-areas** (the long-term target was 100 or more).

## 1. How the world is laid out

- **Ring = region number.** The campaign is a clockwise spiral around the Camp: ring 1 north (Greenvale), ring 2 north (Ashkara, as built), then NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW and back to N at ring 10. Deeper ring means harder, as planned in "long-term world expansion". Slots that are not neighbours need a connector road or a Wayshrine hop (see open decisions).
- **Sub-area rows** each carry: theme and landmark, enemy elements (from variants), a 5-monster roster (bestiary name plus variant), a boss with a gimmick drawn from existing enemy keywords, the one idea it teaches or tests, and a one-line arc (intro, combine, elite test, boss). Nature is Malevolent or Ambivalent only; no yellow or red deities; green-flag gods (Sekhmet, Apep, Sobek, Set, Loki, Charon, Ammit, Thanatos, Persephone, Hades) appear only in Ashkara and the Necropolis, mostly as bosses.
- **Variants are recolours with a twist**: the same base appears in several regions with another element (Kelpie frost in Frostmarch, Salamander magma in Emberreach, Banshee shadow in Gloamfen).
- **Greenvale and Ashkara are kept at 8 sub-areas each.** Their tilesets and maps exist, the 16 sub-areas already cover the first 15 levels, and nothing in the curve needs more. No sub-area is added to either (nothing is marked new there). Regions 3 to 10 are new.

Reaction schedule (all 17 in nine steps): Wildfire, Bloom, Roots, Conduct (1) → Mud, Smother, Fan, Purge, Dusk, Sunfire (2) → Freeze, Steam, Blizzard (3) → Ground (4) → Mist (5) → Pollen (6) → Squall (9). Regions 7, 8 and 10 add none; they combine and revisit. Same-element resonance is met first in Greenvale (2 units), at 4 units from Frostmarch.

Role mixing: pure Attack, Buff, Guard in 1; first hybrids (Guard plus Attack) in 2; Attack plus Buff Heralds in 6; Captains and Attune/Mimic converters in 5 and 7; formation hybrids (Bastion, Echo Drum) in 8; Legendary hubs in 9 and 10.

## 8. Open decisions

1. **Level curve past Lv15.** w(L) at Lv55 is about 36x Lv5 and the enemy HP formula (2.2 + 0.95L) gives about 54; readability on a 5x5 board breaks. Options: keep the formula and add Descend/Heat scaling, flatten enemy HP above Lv20 (HP x 0.5 per level), or shrink the bands to +3 per region.
2. **Adjacency.** Spiral slots are not neighbours (N2 to NE3 to E4 ...); connectors, Wayshrine hops or diagonal gates must be chosen before `build-world.mjs --check`.
3. **Element resistance** on elites and bosses (DESIGN_V2 open decision 19): the Necropolis assumes some (a Shadow boss immune to Dusk), the rest of the world needs none.
4. **Plain monsters carry an element** here (every roster entry has a variant element except a few plain ones); confirm decision 18 (all enemies have one).
5. **Greenvale and Ashkara stay at 8.** If the user wants 10 each, candidates are a Greenvale coast and an Ashkara sphinx plateau (new art needed).
6. **Green-flag gods as bosses** (Sekhmet, Apep, Set, Loki, Charon, Ammit, Hades): keep, or swap for monsters (Hydra, Mummy, Garmr).
7. **Boss familiarity:** 77 at ①-③, 23 at ④-⑤. The ④-⑤ bosses are mostly Maya, Andean, Polynesian and Finnish creatures chosen for culture spread.
8. **Cultural care:** the Necropolis mixes living-religion afterlives (Mictlan, Jeoseung) with ancient ones; Mictlan and Jeoseung rows use folklore creatures (La Llorona, Gwisin) and no deities.
9. **Art budget:** regions 3 to 10 are 84 new sub-areas in 8 new tilesets: about 4 to 6 MB on the single build (cap 9 MB, so the cap or the packing must change), plus interior kits for the castle and the Necropolis.
10. **Fenrir, Kraken, Vodyanoy, Bastet** (art dropped) are avoided; sea and ice bosses use Charybdis, Jörmungandr, Garmr and Kelpie instead.
