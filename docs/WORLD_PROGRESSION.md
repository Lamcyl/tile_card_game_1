# World Progression: Regions 1 to 10

Status: proposal (**V2.1**: 7 elements, 12 reactions, 5 families, big enemies, level rule to Lv55). Design data only; no game code changed. Data: `docs/data/regions.json` (every region and sub-area has a stable `id` and `"status": "proposed"`). Check: `node docs/data/check-regions.mjs` (every roster and boss base name exists in `BESTIARY.md`, is Malevolent or Ambivalent, and is not in "Removed for sensitivity").

Inputs: DESIGN_V2.md (V2.1: units, 7 elements, 12 reactions, 5 families, Rend, big enemies), BESTIARY.md (variants, families, terrain index), ENEMY_REVAMP.md Part B (Greenvale and Ashkara battle scripts), GAME_DESIGN.md 24.8 (level rule), IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md (8 compass directions, structure regions). Counts: **10 regions, 100 sub-areas**, each with an elite and a boss; 30 big elites and 56 big bosses (23 in the boss row).

## 1. How the world is laid out

- **Ring = region number.** The campaign is a clockwise spiral around the Camp: ring 1 north (Greenvale), ring 2 north (Ashkara, as built), then NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW and back to N at ring 10. Deeper ring means harder, as planned in "long-term world expansion". Slots that are not neighbours need a connector road or a Wayshrine hop (see open decisions).
- **Sub-area rows** each carry: theme and landmark, enemy elements (from variants; frost = Water/Ice, storm = Wind/Lightning), a 5-monster roster (bestiary name plus variant), an elite and a boss with a footprint (WxD) and, for some bosses, a boss-row flag, and a boss gimmick drawn from existing enemy keywords, the one idea it teaches or tests, and a one-line arc (intro, combine, elite test, boss). Nature is Malevolent or Ambivalent only; no yellow or red deities; green-flag gods (Sekhmet, Apep, Sobek, Set, Loki, Charon, Ammit, Thanatos, Persephone, Hades) appear only in Ashkara and the Necropolis, mostly as bosses.
- **Variants are recolours with a twist**: the same base appears in several regions with another element (Kelpie frost in Frostmarch, Salamander magma in Emberreach, Banshee shadow in Gloamfen).
- **Greenvale and Ashkara are kept at 8 sub-areas each.** Their tilesets and maps exist, the 16 sub-areas already cover the first 15 levels, and nothing in the curve needs more. No sub-area is added to either (nothing is marked new there). Regions 3 to 10 are new.

### 4.1 Enemy stat rule to Lv55 (and the player power it assumes)

Goal: fights stay readable with small numbers. Enemy numbers stay under 100 for 1x1 bodies and under about 160 for the largest bosses; a basic enemy always costs about 3.4 hits.

**Enemy formulas (basic 1x1, rounded half up).** `L <= 15`: HP = 2.2 + 0.95 L, ATK = 1.5 + 0.42 L (unchanged). `L > 15`: **HP = 16.5 + 0.5 (L - 15), ATK = 7.8 + 0.12 (L - 15).** Elite HP x1.6, boss HP x2.1, then the footprint factor (DESIGN_V2.md 10.5). Past Lv15 an enemy gains about 1 HP every 2 levels and 1 ATK every 8 levels.

**Player expectation.** Expected damage per attacker hit P(L) = 2 + 0.2 L for `L <= 15`, then 5 + 0.14 (L - 15). Hero HP H(L) = 24 + 0.9 (L - 15) past Lv15. A squad of 6 attackers deals about 6 P per turn.

Rules that keep it stable: (1) item-level numbers stop growing at iLv15 (x2.0), so deeper loot gets better by rarity roll chance and sub-effects (DESIGN_V2.md 9.1), not by bigger numbers; (2) run upgrades are capped by the deepest level reached, as today; (3) Armor and Hide thresholds stay at 1-3 for all levels (they do not scale); (4) Shields grow slowly: block 2-3 at Lv15, 4-5 at Lv55 (including sub-effects), so a lane of a wide enemy (ATK 7-8 at Lv55 for a 2x1 boss) is mostly absorbed by one Shield; (5) the sim target is unchanged: a fight is won in 1-2 turns, a boss in 2-3.

**Rewards.** w(L) = (L/5)^1.5 is kept up to Lv20 (8.0). Past Lv20 it is an open decision (section 8): 36x at Lv55 breaks the Souls economy even though the enemy numbers no longer explode.

## 8. Open decisions

1. **Reward curve past Lv20.** The enemy and player stat rule is set (4.1). w(L) = (L/5)^1.5 reaches 36x at Lv55; options: cap the exponent after Lv20, or flatten Souls and Gold per fight while keeping region rewards as they are. Needs the campaign sim.
2. **Adjacency.** Spiral slots are not neighbours (N2 to NE3 to E4 ...); connectors, Wayshrine hops or diagonal gates must be chosen before `build-world.mjs --check`.
3. **Element resistance** on elites and bosses (DESIGN_V2 open decision 11): the Necropolis assumes some (a Shadow boss immune to Eclipse), the rest of the world needs none.
4. **Plain monsters carry an element** here (every roster entry has a variant element except a few plain ones); confirm (DESIGN_V2 open decision 12).
5. **Greenvale and Ashkara stay at 8.** If the user wants 10 each, candidates are a Greenvale coast and an Ashkara sphinx plateau (new art needed).
6. **Green-flag gods as bosses** (Sekhmet, Apep, Set, Loki, Charon, Ammit, Hades): keep, or swap for monsters (Hydra, Mummy, Garmr).
7. **Boss familiarity:** 77 at ①-③, 23 at ④-⑤.
8. **Cultural care:** the Necropolis mixes living-religion afterlives with ancient ones; Mictlan and Jeoseung rows use folklore creatures and no deities.
9. **Art budget:** regions 3 to 10 are 84 new sub-areas in 8 new tilesets, about 4 to 6 MB on the single build (cap 9 MB); big-body art adds 2x1, 1x2, 2x2 and 3x1 crops for 30 elites and 42 bosses.
10. **Secret bosses:** one per region behind a Sealed Cache needing 2 Keys is proposed (DESIGN_V2 9.3); they are not among the 100 sub-areas yet.
11. **Boss-row count:** 23 bosses sit in the boss row; confirm the gate (DESIGN_V2 10.4) is fun in playtest before more are added.
12. **Fenrir, Kraken, Vodyanoy, Bastet** (art dropped) are avoided; sea and ice bosses use Charybdis, Jörmungandr, Garmr and Kelpie instead.
